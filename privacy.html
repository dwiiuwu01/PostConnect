Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PostConnect handles information when you use our website.

Information We Access

PostConnect may access basic TikTok account information and the permissions required to upload or publish video content through TikTok's official APIs.

How We Use Information

The information is used only to connect your TikTok account and provide the posting functionality requested by you.

Video Content

Video content selected by the user is used only for the purpose of posting it to the user's TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Data Sharing

PostConnect does not sell or rent users' personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide the requested TikTok integration.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application.

Third-Party Services

PostConnect uses TikTok services and APIs. TikTok may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the PostConnect team.
